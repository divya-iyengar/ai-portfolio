# MIT Auto-ID Laboratory

## Role

Summer Research Intern
MIT Auto-ID Laboratory
Mentor: Dr. Sanjay Sarma
Summer 2015 - Summer 2017 (three consecutive summer reseach internships)

## Summary 

During high school, I spent three consecutive summers as a research intern at the MIT Auto-ID Laboratory, where I worked alongside graduate students on a different engineering project each year. These internships provided my first exposure to university research, multidisciplinary engineering, and collaborative technical problem solving. 

Working within a graduate research environment allowed me learn directly from researchers while gaining practical experience across mechanical design, embedded systems, software development, additive manufacturing, and early cloud-connected applications. At the conclusion of each summer, I presented my work and project outcomes to faculty members and research staff. 

Although each internship focused on a different technical challenge, together they established the engineering foundation that ultimately led me toward robotics and applied AI research. 

## Summer 2015 - Digital Reconstruction and Additive Manufacturing

My first project focused on recreating the original Bosworth campus buildings from historical MIT archives as part of a commemorative initiative celebrating the university's campus history.

Responsibilities included:
- Reconstructing historic buildings using SketchUp based on archival drawings and references
- Preparing models for additive manufacturing
- Producing miniature 3D-printed building models
- Applying copper finishes using a sputtering process to create presentation pieces for faculty

This project introduced me to engineering design workflows, CAD modeling, rapid prototyping, and manufacturing processes.

## Summer 2016 - IoT Water Pressure Monitoring

During my second internship, I worked on an early Internet of Things (IoT) monitoring system designed to measure water pressure within a pipe valve.

My work included:
- Developing SQL-based data storage for sensor measurements
- Interfacing with Arduino hardware to collect pressure data
- Monitoring sensor readings through a web-based dashboard (phpMyAdmin)
- Exploring how embedded devices could communicate with cloud-connected software systems

This experience introduced me to embedded programming, sensor integration, databases, and connected devices.

## Summer 2017 - Voice-Controlled Robotic Head

My final internship focused on integrating speech processing with robotic actuation.

The project involved: 
- Designing and 3D printing a robotic head
- Developing software that responded to spoken commands
- Using AWS Lambda to process verbal input and trigger robotic movement
- Integrating mechanical, software, and cloud-based components into a functional prototype

This was my first experience working on a complete mechatronic system that combined hardware, cloud services, and software into a single interactive application.

## Lessons Learned

Looking back, these internships shaped my interest in engineering long before I entered college. Working in a lab with exposure to graduate researchers showed me how complex systems are built through collaboration across multiple disciplines, while the variety of projects exposed me to mechanical design, software development, embedded systems, and cloud technologies.

At the end of each summer, I would present my findings to the professor. He would ask me to whiteboard different systems live or explain the biggest challenges to push my knowledge. That experience gave me confidence working in research environments and sparked my long-term interest in robotics, intelligent systems, and applying software to solve real-world engineering problems.
